First time here? A few useful things.

Connect. Use Chrome on Android, or Chrome / Edge on Windows or macOS. Power on the printer, close Funny Print, then choose SWS-PT1 in the Bluetooth dialog. Safari and Firefox cannot connect.

Print. The preview shows your artwork in its intended proportions. Paper-feed correction adds rows during printing to compensate for the printer’s vertical squeeze. End spacing controls the extra blank paper for tearing. Keep this tab open while printing.

Reconnect. This printer sleeps after inactivity. If it disappears, switch it on and connect again. Stopping a transfer cannot recall lines already buffered by the printer.
